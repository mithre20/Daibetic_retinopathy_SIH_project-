# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General FAQs

### Q1: What is the main objective of AI DR Screen?
The objective is to eliminate preventable blindness caused by Diabetic Retinopathy in rural and semi-urban India through accessible, explainable, and rapid frontline screening triage at Primary Health Centers.

### Q2: Can Diabetic Retinopathy be cured?
While retinal damage cannot always be completely reversed, diabetic retinopathy can be effectively treated, halted, and managed with laser photocoagulation, anti-VEGF injections, surgery, and strict glycemic and blood pressure control, especially when caught early.

### Q3: How often should diabetic patients get their eyes checked?
Every person with diabetes should receive a dilated retinal examination at least once every year, or more frequently (every 3 to 6 months) if retinopathy has already been detected.

### Q4: Does the AI replace doctors?
No. AI DR Screen acts as an assistant and triage filter for medical officers and eye care specialists. It does not replace ophthalmologists.

### Q5: Does the chatbot give medical advice?
No. The chatbot provides platform navigation, answers technical and procedural questions, explains screening outputs and referral rules, but never provides personal medical diagnoses, prescriptions, or treatment advice.

### Q6: What languages are supported?
The chatbot supports English, Tamil (தமிழ்), and Hindi (हिंदी).
