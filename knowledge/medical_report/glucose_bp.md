# Blood Glucose and Blood Pressure Parameters

## What is Blood Glucose?
Blood glucose measures the concentration of sugar (glucose) circulating in the bloodstream. Maintaining near-normal blood glucose is the primary clinical factor in slowing the onset and progression of Diabetic Retinopathy.

## Glycemic Indices Explained:
1. **FBS / FBG (Fasting Blood Sugar / Glucose)**: Measured after an overnight fast of at least 8 hours. Standard reference: Normal < 100 mg/dL; Prediabetes 100–125 mg/dL; Diabetes >= 126 mg/dL.
2. **PPBS (Postprandial Blood Sugar)**: Measured exactly 2 hours after the start of a meal. Standard reference: Normal < 140 mg/dL; Prediabetes 140–199 mg/dL; Diabetes >= 200 mg/dL.
3. **RBS / RBG (Random Blood Sugar)**: Measured at any time of day regardless of when food was last eaten. Values >= 200 mg/dL accompanied by classic symptoms indicate diabetes.
4. **HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin)**: Reflects average blood sugar levels over the past 2 to 3 months. Standard reference: Normal < 5.7%; Prediabetes 5.7%–6.4%; Diabetes >= 6.5%. For diabetic patients, a target < 7.0% is commonly recommended by diabetologists.

## What is Blood Pressure (BP)?
Blood pressure measures the hydrostatic pressure exerted by circulating blood upon arterial walls.
- **Systolic Blood Pressure (top number)**: Pressure when the heart beats.
- **Diastolic Blood Pressure (bottom number)**: Pressure when the heart rests between beats.
- Standard guideline: Normal < 120/80 mmHg; Hypertension Stage 1: 130–139 / 80–89 mmHg; Hypertension Stage 2: >= 140 / >= 90 mmHg.

## CRITICAL SAFETY RULE: Retinal Scans Do NOT Measure Blood Sugar or BP
**Can the retinal fundus image directly measure glucose or blood pressure?**
**ABSOLUTELY NOT.** The digital retinal scan analyzes vascular micro-lesions in the eye. It **cannot** measure chemical glucose concentrations in the blood or hydrostatic arterial pressure. Any glucose or blood pressure figures shown in the application come strictly from uploaded laboratory reports or manual user input.

## Can the Chatbot Diagnose Diabetes or Hypertension?
**NO.** The chatbot and application never diagnose systemic diabetes or hypertension from single isolated numbers. Reference ranges printed on the certified lab report must be interpreted by a treating physician or diabetologist.
