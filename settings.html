ℹ️ About AI DR Screen (XAIRIS)

Explainable AI for Diabetic Retinopathy Screening in Rural India.

AI DR Screen — XAIRIS

Production Clinical Release • Version 2.6.4

AI DR Screen is an explainable artificial intelligence tele-ophthalmology platform developed to prevent avoidable blindness across rural India through early detection of Diabetic Retinopathy. The system combines high-accuracy deep convolutional neural networks with transparent visual explanations (Grad-CAM heatmaps & U-Net lesion segmentation), ensuring primary healthcare workers and clinical specialists can collaborate with trust.

Primary Neural Network EfficientNet-B0 (Transfer Learning)
Comparative Benchmark ResNet50 Architecture
Lesion Segmentation U-Net Architecture
Explainability Methods Grad-CAM + SHAP Feature Attribution
Evaluation Datasets EyePACS • APTOS 2019 • Rural India PHC Cohort
